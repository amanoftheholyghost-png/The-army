Who We Are

A Church Built on Love

Grace Community Church has been a beacon of hope in our city for over 25 years. We are a family of believers dedicated to worshiping God, serving our community, and sharing the love of Christ with everyone we meet.

Whether you're exploring faith for the first time, returning after time away, or looking for a church home — you are welcome here. Come as you are.
